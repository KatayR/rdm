ffmpeg.exe in RDM's folder (on a Mac: ffmpeg in the app's Contents/MacOS) is FFmpeg 9.0.2
(https://ffmpeg.org), built for RDM from the unchanged official source. RDM runs it as a
separate program, only to copy video and audio streams into a file; nothing of FFmpeg is
linked into RDM's own programs.

Licence: GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later. Its text is next to this
file (ffmpeg-LGPL-2.1.txt). The build has no GPL and no non-free parts (no --enable-gpl,
no --enable-nonfree).

Source: ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz, as published at https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ . The same file
is attached to every release of RDM, beside the setup:
https://github.com/KatayR/rdm/releases

How it was built (the exact configure flags and why each part is there, the compiler, the
checks of the downloads): the folder "ffmpeg" of RDM's source, at the tag of this version
of RDM (https://github.com/KatayR/rdm). The flags are also printed by:  ffmpeg.exe -version

To use another build of FFmpeg, replace that file with it. What RDM needs
of it is listed in ffmpeg/build_ffmpeg.sh and ffmpeg/README.md of the source; a build that
lacks a part can write files that do not play.
